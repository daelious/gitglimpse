# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming experience, we pledge to make participation in gitglimpse and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include harassment, abusive language, and discrimination.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue on GitHub or contacting the project maintainers.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project.
